Introduction Last updated: 2019-06-01

Welcome to the world of pathfinding visualizer, where the power of algorithms meets the beauty of visualisation. In this project, we embark on a journey to explore the fascinating realm of pathfinding algorithms and witness their incredible ability to find the optimal paths amidst intricate mazes and labyrinths. Pathfinding algorithms are at the heart of numerous applications, from navigation systems guiding us through busy streets to optimising logistics and planning in various fields. By simulating these algorithms in a visualizer, we can grasp their inner workings and witness their effectiveness firsthand. Our pathfinding visualizer project provides an interactive platform that allows you to create custom mazes, define starting and target points, and watch as algorithms effortlessly navigate the maze to find the shortest or most efficient path. Whether you are a computer science enthusiast, a student learning about algorithms, or simply intrigued by the mechanics behind finding the optimal routes, this visualizer offers an engaging and educational experience. With an array of algorithms at your disposal, including classics like Dijkstra's algorithm, A* search, and breadth-first search, you can compare their performance, understand their strengths and weaknesses, and gain a deeper understanding of their underlying principles. Each algorithm has its unique approach to exploring the maze, and through visualisation, you can witness the intricate dance of nodes and edges as they traverse the path, making decisions along the way. But it doesn't stop there! Our visualizer provides additional features such as obstacle creation, enabling you to test the algorithms' ability to navigate around obstacles and adapt to changing conditions. You can adjust the maze size, customise colors and visual elements, and dive deep into the realm of pathfinding, experimenting with different scenarios and observing the algorithms' behaviour in real-time. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced programmer, our pathfinding visualizer offers a captivating and enlightening journey into the world of algorithms. So join us now as we unravel the secrets of pathfinding and witness the magic of algorithms coming to life before your eyes. Let's embark on this adventure together and unlock the hidden paths that await us!

Technology stack used

Our technology stack for this project comprises HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript, forming a powerful combination that enables us to create dynamic and visually appealing web applications.

  • HTML (HyperText Markup Language) serves as the foundation of our project, providing the structure and semantics for our web pages. It allows us to define the various elements and their relationships, including headings, paragraphs, lists, images, forms, and more. With HTML, we can create a well-organized and accessible structure for our content.
  • CSS (Cascading Style Sheets) enhances the presentation and aesthetics of our web pages. It enables us to define the layout, colors, fonts, and other visual aspects, ensuring a consistent and visually pleasing experience for our users. By separating the content from the design, CSS promotes modularity and maintainability, making it easier to update and customize the appearance of our application.
  • Bootstrap is a popular CSS framework that adds an extra layer of power and flexibility to our project. It provides a comprehensive set of pre-designed components and responsive grid systems that allow us to quickly build responsive and mobile-friendly interfaces. With Bootstrap, we can effortlessly create responsive navigation menus, buttons, forms, modals, and more, saving valuable development time while ensuring a consistent and professional look and feel across different devices.
  • JavaScript, the programming language of the web, brings interactivity and dynamism to our project. With JavaScript, we can implement complex logic, handle user interactions, and dynamically modify the content and behavior of our web pages. It allows us to respond to events, manipulate the DOM (Document Object Model), make asynchronous requests to servers, and seamlessly integrate with other technologies and APIs.
    • Together, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript form a robust technology stack that empowers us to create engaging and interactive web applications. They provide the necessary tools and capabilities to deliver an intuitive user experience, responsive design, and a visually appealing interface. By harnessing the potential of these technologies, we can build powerful and user-friendly applications that captivate and delight our users.
